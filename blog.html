PERSONAL BLOG

Why UNTOLD Is More Than Just a Festival

I’ve been to many festivals over the years, but UNTOLD has a completely different kind of energy. Every time I arrive in Cluj and step into the stadium, it feels like entering another world. The lights, the music, and the people create an atmosphere that you truly cannot understand until you experience it yourself.

What I love most is the perfect combination of incredible production and the amazing vibe of the crowd. Whether you come for the world-famous DJs or simply for the experience, the festival manages to make you forget reality for a few unforgettable days.

Every year I write down my favorite moments in this blog: the best live sets, breathtaking stages, creative festival outfits, and the unforgettable nightlife atmosphere of Cluj.

“UNTOLD is not only about music. It’s about emotions, people, and memories that stay with you long after the festival ends.”
